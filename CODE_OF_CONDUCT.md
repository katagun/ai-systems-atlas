# Code of conduct

## Standard

This project exists to keep a directory accurate, not to referee people. Be direct about technical and editorial disagreements — including a rejected candidate, a disputed score, or a reverted claim — and respectful about everything else. Back a claim with evidence rather than repetition, and accept a maintainer's settled call unless new evidence changes it.

Unacceptable: harassment, personal attacks, discriminatory language or imagery, doxxing, sexualized content, and sustained disruption of an issue, pull request, or discussion.

## Scope

Applies to this repository's issues, pull requests, discussions, and any other space where someone represents the project.

## Enforcement

Report privately to `2429776+katagun@users.noreply.github.com` with what happened, where, and any relevant links. Reports are handled by the maintainer, kept as confidential as the situation allows, and may lead to a warning, edited or removed content, or a temporary or permanent ban from the repository. Retaliation against a good-faith report is itself a violation.

Suspected security vulnerabilities follow [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md), not this file.
