How it is organized
The organization's own about page says it is "an AI and robotics company based in Palo Alto, California", which is the headquarters statement the record uses; the same page records that it was founded in 2014 as Halodi Robotics by Bernt Øyvind Børnich, so the company is Norwegian-founded and American-based, and the record says so rather than choosing a country for the origin, because `headquarters` is where the organization says it is based and nothing here is a claim about where it began. It is an AI company because the AI is the product: its own site separates an "AI" section from the robots, names Redwood as its world model, and describes NEO as its flagship product, so the organization develops both the robot and the model that runs it. It is recorded on the robot rather than a release because the catalog reviews no Redwood release, and Redwood is documented as a world model that plans actions rather than as a language release, which is outside the Models collection on the ground the robotics scope records. The reviewed robot is NEO, the home robot the record covers, not the earlier EVE industrial line, which the about page describes as deployed in customer factories from 2022. No parent organization is recorded: the pages read name no holding or controlling entity. Its site names no frontier-safety, responsible-scaling, or preparedness framework, so the field is omitted.
