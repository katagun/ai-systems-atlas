How it is organized
The legal notice names the site publisher as Pollen Robotics SAS at 2 Place Jean Jaurès, 33000 Bordeaux, France, and the about page says "Pollen Robotics is based in the beautiful city of Bordeaux, South-West of France" and that it was founded in 2016 by former Inria researchers, so the headquarters is France. It is recorded on the reviewed robot reachy-mini rather than on a release because the catalog reviews no model release of its own: the voice model its Reachy Mini page says powers the robot's built-in conversation is not named. No parent organization is recorded: neither the legal notice, the about page nor the terms of sale name one. Its site names no frontier-safety or scaling framework, so the field is omitted.
