How it is organized
The Chinese about page's milestones say "逐际动力 LimX Dynamics 正式成立，全球总部位于中国深圳", that LimX Dynamics was officially founded with its global headquarters in Shenzhen, China, and the English about page's milestones say it "was founded in Shenzhen". The after-sales policy names LimX Dynamics Technology Co.,Ltd. as the seller, so this is one organization and the record names no parent. It is recorded on the reviewed robot limx-tron-1 rather than on a release because the catalog reviews no model release of its own: its pages describe the LimX COSA humanoid brain system and the FluxVLA Engine, a VLA engineering platform, none of which is a model release in the Models collection. Its site names no frontier-safety or scaling framework, so the field is omitted.
