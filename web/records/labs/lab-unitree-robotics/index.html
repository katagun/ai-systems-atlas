How it is organized
The contact page names a Hangzhou Headquarters at 3rd Floor, Building 1, Fengda Creative Park, No. 88 Dongliu Road, Binjiang District, Hangzhou City, Zhejiang Province, China, with a Shenzhen office and a Shanghai entity, Shanghai Gaoyi Technology Co., Ltd., given by name; the record takes the Hangzhou address because the page itself labels it the headquarters and names the other two as an office and a differently named entity, and the record notes them here rather than treating three addresses as three headquarters. Its own about page calls it a "civilian robotics company" focused on the R&D, production, and sales of general-purpose legged and humanoid robots, says it has worked on that since 2017, and states that it self-researchs the motors, reducers, controllers, LIDAR, and perception and motion-control algorithms its robots use, which is why the organization is an AI company rather than a hardware vendor that buys its intelligence: the software is developed in house and the catalog reviews three of its robots. It is recorded on those robots rather than on a release because the catalog reviews no model of its own, and the UnifoLM-X1-0 embodied intelligence model its about page describes is a policy that acts on a robot, which the robotics scope keeps out of the Models collection; the record says so rather than implying the organization publishes no model. No parent organization is recorded: the pages read name no holding entity. Its site names no frontier-safety, responsible-scaling, or preparedness framework, so the field is omitted.
