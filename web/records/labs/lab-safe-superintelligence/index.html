How it is organized
Safe Superintelligence Inc., which calls itself an American company with offices in Palo Alto and Tel Aviv, states on its own site that it started "the world's first straight-shot SSI lab, with one goal and one product: a safe superintelligence", and that "SSI is our mission, our name, and our entire product roadmap". The pages name no legal form beyond the Inc. in the site title and footer, no registered address, no directors, and no governing terms; there is no privacy policy, terms of service, model catalog, release-notes page, or safety framework, and the /safety path returns not-found. Its GitHub organization exists and publishes no repositories at all, and it has no Hugging Face organization, so neither is listed as a channel: a channel is a page or organization a reader can watch for the lab's work, and an empty account is not one. It is recorded on its own published statement of intent rather than on a release, which is why it joins to nothing in this collection; the safety language on its site is a mission statement rather than a framework, so no framework is recorded. Its own updates page names Ilya Sutskever as CEO and Daniel Levy as President as of July 2025 and records a July 2026 NVIDIA partnership. The organization's own site says it is "an American company with offices in Palo Alto and Tel Aviv, where we have deep roots and the ability to recruit top technical talent", so the work is not only where the American entity is: Tel Aviv is the second office the record names.
