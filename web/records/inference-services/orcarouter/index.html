Boundary
Represents the hosted OrcaRouter routing service at api.orcarouter.ai, operated by CONTINUUM AI PTE. LTD. (Singapore). It is not the upstream providers it forwards to, which keep their own terms and retention, and not OrcaRouter-Lite, the vendor's separate MIT-licensed self-hosted gateway edition, even though a Lite deployment can register the hosted service as one provider in its routing chain. The published MCP server is an optional client tool, not this service boundary.
