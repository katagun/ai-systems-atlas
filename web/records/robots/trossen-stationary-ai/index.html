Trossen Stationary AI

Trossen Robotics' bench-mounted bimanual machine-learning workstation, sold as the Stationary AI: two leader-follower pairs of WidowX AI arms with four Intel RealSense D405 cameras, a 10-inch touchscreen and a high-performance desktop, which the maker describes as an all-in-one station for data collection and model development. The maker's documentation walks through training Pi0 and Pi 0.5 policies with its arms under the openpi framework and running inference, with API and SDK support.
