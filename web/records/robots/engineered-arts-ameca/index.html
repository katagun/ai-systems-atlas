Ameca

Engineered Arts' full-size social humanoid robot with a lifelike face and a 61-degree-of-freedom body, run by the company's Tritium software suite. Engineered Arts names no learned model that drives the robot's motion; its specifications list default Tritium AI integrations with OpenAI ChatGPT, OpenAI Whisper and Amazon Poly, and its Tritium AI page says the Pro agent platform can use a range of LLMs or a custom model.
