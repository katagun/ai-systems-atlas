LimX TRON 1

LimX Dynamics' two-legged robot, which its own pages call "The First Multi-Modal Biped Robot", sold in Standard and EDU editions with a foot end that switches between point-foot, sole and wheeled modes. It has no torso or arms in its base configuration. LimX names no learned model for it; its repositories document training reinforcement-learning locomotion policies for the TRON1, deploying ONNX policies to it, and driving it from an LLM voice agent.
