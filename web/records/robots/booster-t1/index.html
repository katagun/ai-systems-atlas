Booster T1

Booster Robotics' small humanoid development platform, listed on its product page as "~1.2m, ~30kg, 23-41 DoFs" and sold in Basic, Standard and Customized configurations. Booster names no learned model for it; its developer guide documents Python and C++ SDKs for sending control commands, and its open-source repositories document training reinforcement-learning locomotion policies and deploying exported policies to a physical T1.
