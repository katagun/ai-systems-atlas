DEEP Robotics Lite3

DEEP Robotics' compact quadruped robot dog, sold as the Lite3 in Basic, Venture, Pro and LIDAR versions. DEEP Robotics names no learned model for it; its product page lists secondary development with a motion-control SDK, and its repositories document training a reinforcement-learning locomotion policy for the Lite3 and deploying it to the physical robot.
