Reachy Mini

Pollen Robotics' desktop robot with a movable head, two antennas and a rotating body, sold as a kit in Wireless and Lite versions. It has no arms, legs or wheeled base. Pollen names no learned model for the robot itself; its documentation says it "is designed for AI Builders" and documents an SDK, a REST and WebSocket API, and a reference conversation app in which an LLM calls tools that move the robot.
