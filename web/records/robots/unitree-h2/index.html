Unitree H2

Unitree's full-size humanoid robot, sold as the H2 and the H2 EDU. Unitree names no learned model for it; its product page says a 2070 TOPS chip supports diverse intelligent models, and its reinforcement-learning repository lists the H2 among the robots it supports and registers velocity-tracking tasks for it.
