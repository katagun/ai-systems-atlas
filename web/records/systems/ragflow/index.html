Product boundary
The record covers the Apache-2.0 repository, which ships both products RAGFlow documents separately: Chat, "suitable for applications mainly based on knowledge base Q&A and multi-turn dialogue", and Agent, "suitable for business workflows requiring conditional branching, tool calling, data processing or multi-step orchestration". The vendor's own cloud service at cloud.ragflow.io is a separate hosted product whose terms this record does not review.
