Ternary Bonsai 2 27B

Prism ML's Bonsai 2 27B, a ternary-weight (1.72 bits per weight) build of Alibaba's Qwen3.8-27B that fits in about 5.95 GB, keeps the 262,144-token context and image input, and ships as Apache-2.0 GGUF files that need Prism ML's llama.cpp fork.
