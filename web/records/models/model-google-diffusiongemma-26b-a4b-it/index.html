Model · Multimodal language model

DiffusionGemma 26B A4B IT

Google DeepMind's ungated experimental open-weights multimodal model that generates text by discrete diffusion rather than token-by-token autoregression: an encoder-decoder over the 26B-total, 3.8B-active Gemma 4 mixture-of-experts architecture, denoising 256-token canvases with bidirectional attention for 256K-token context, released under Apache-2.0 and served on Vertex AI Model Garden.
